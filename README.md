# Shifu

### Learn to Think, Not Just to Get Answers

A learning Skill that helps you understand deeply, solve independently,
and gradually become able to walk farther on your own.

### 学会思考，而不只是得到答案

一个帮助你深入理解、独立解决问题，
并最终能够独自走得更远的学习 Skill。

## 中文

这个世界上没有任何一个方法，没有任何一种工具，没有任何一个人能够逼你去学习，能够让你想去学习。真正能让你有这个内驱力的，只有你自己。

这个 Skill 不是为了那些想得到答案、想走捷径、抄近道，减少思索时的酸楚与痛苦，把自己麻痹在各种看似繁忙的行动中的人。从古至今，这样的人有无数方法去达成他们的目标：在古时候，是写在贴身衣物上的蝇头小楷，是千两白银换取的一纸答案；在现在，是举过头顶躲避金属探测仪的手机，也是一个上传题目就可以获得所有答案和解析的 AI。这个 Skill 不是为这些人准备的，没有什么东西可以帮到他们。

那这个 Skill 是做什么的呢？

它是在你愿意学习时，让你学得更有效、理解得更深刻、掌握得更全面的工具。

我希望它能够在你一脸茫然的时候，指出向前踏第一步的方向；在你因思索而无比痛苦的时候，轻轻一点，引导你搭建一座行至彼岸的桥梁。它不会因为可以直接给你答案，就夺走你本来应该完成的思考。

我希望它是一个师傅，带你叩开你想探索的学问之门。

当你再度遇到曾经那个让你茫然或痛苦的知识时，你的嘴角会挂起一抹自信的微笑，而它会站在远处，目送你渐行渐远。

## English

Nothing in this world—no method, no tool, no person—can force you to learn or make you want to learn. The drive to do so can come only from within yourself.

This Skill is not made for those who merely want answers, who search for shortcuts and easier paths, who wish to escape the ache and struggle of thinking, or who numb themselves with endless activities that only appear productive. Throughout history, such people have always found ways to achieve their aims. In ancient times, it might have been lines of tiny script hidden inside their clothing, or a sheet of answers bought with a thousand taels of silver. Today, it might be a phone held above the head to evade a metal detector, or an AI that provides every answer and explanation as soon as a question is uploaded.

This Skill is not made for them. Nothing can truly help them.

Then what is this Skill for?

It is a tool for those moments when you are willing to learn—helping you learn more effectively, understand more deeply, and develop a more complete command of what you study.

When you are utterly lost, I hope it can point you toward the first step forward. When the pain of thinking becomes almost unbearable, I hope it can offer the lightest, most carefully placed touch—guiding you as you build your own bridge to the other shore. It will not take away the thinking that was always yours to do simply because it is capable of giving you the answer.

I hope it can be a Shifu, leading you to the doorway of the knowledge you wish to explore.

And when you one day encounter again the knowledge that once left you lost or in pain, a quiet, confident smile will appear on your face.

The Skill will stand in the distance, watching as you walk farther and farther on your own.
